Maglev-Assisted Takeoff

Maglev-Assisted Takeoff

Controllable magnetic runway lanes plus passive landing-gear magnetic elements: a conservative, fail-safe proposal for reducing wheel load during the takeoff ground run.

Short version

Kratek povzetek

BD proposes segmented magnetic runway lanes and candidate rings or plates near the landing gear. The target is a 5–20% reduction in tire normal load during a defined early takeoff segment. Upward force, net energy benefit and safe dynamics have not been measured. The proposed 1–3 cm dimension still needs defined reference surfaces; tire contact must remain.

BD predlaga segmentirane magnetne pasove v stezi ter možne obroče ali plošče ob podvozju. Cilj je za 5–20 % manjša normalna sila na pnevmatikah v določenem zgodnjem delu vzletnega zaleta. Sila navzgor, neto energijska korist in varna dinamika še niso izmerjene. Za predlagano mero 1–3 cm je treba določiti referenčni površini; stik pnevmatik s stezo mora ostati.

BD's proposed move

Target wheel-load-dependent losses without changing airborne aircraft design or pilot technique. Prior-art review is pending.

First test

Specify the field and gear-element geometry, then run a loaded wheel truck over a 5–10 m instrumented lane. Measure vertical force, magnetic drag, electrical input, heat and tire contact across speed and gap.

Failure mode

If lift is unstable, EMI is unmanageable, or energy/cost overwhelms savings, the branch is revised or killed.

BD-jev predlog

Ciljati izgube, ki so odvisne od obremenitve koles, brez spremembe letala v zraku ali tehnike pilota. Pregled sorodnega dela še čaka.

Prvi test

Določiti geometrijo polja in elementa na podvozju, nato voditi obremenjen kolesni voziček prek 5–10 m instrumentiranega pasu. Pri različnih hitrostih in režah meriti navpično silo, magnetni upor, električni vhod, toploto in stik pnevmatik.

Način padca

Če je dvig nestabilen, EMI neobvladljiv ali strošek/energija preseže prihranke, se veja spremeni ali opusti.

Concept overview

Pregled koncepta

Runway magnetic lane — simplified sketch
Magnetni pas v stezi — poenostavljena skica
✈

Runway magnetic lanes

Flush, segmented linear modules are embedded in the first 2–2.5 km of a runway. Zone lengths can range from roughly 25–100 m and are ramped smoothly to avoid step changes.

Aircraft integration

Candidate rings or plates would sit in wheel hubs or axle carriers. Material, magnetization, geometry and force direction remain unspecified; a passive ferromagnetic plate could be attracted toward the runway. New aircraft might offer room to optimize wheel count, bogie spacing and thermal management.

Dual-mode operation

At equipped airports MAT energizes only for identified departures. At all other airports, the aircraft behaves exactly like today.

Operational boundary

The proposed plan ramps the field down before V1. Rejected takeoffs during the active segment and field failures still require a braking and directional-control safety case.

Magnetni pasovi v stezi

Poravnani, segmentirani linearni moduli so vgrajeni v prvih 2–2,5 km steze. Cone so lahko dolge približno 25–100 m in imajo mehke prehode.

Integracija na letalu

Možni obroči ali plošče bi bili v pestih koles ali nosilcih osi. Material, magnetizacija, geometrija in smer sile še niso določeni; pasivno feromagnetno ploščo bi polje lahko privlačilo proti stezi. Nova letala bi morda omogočila prilagoditev števila koles, razmika bogijev in odvajanja toplote.

Dvojni način delovanja

Na opremljenih letališčih se MAT vklopi samo za prepoznane odhode. Na vseh drugih letališčih letalo deluje kot danes.

Operativna meja

Načrt predvideva izklop polja pred V1. Zavrnjen vzlet med aktivnim segmentom in napake polja še potrebujejo varnostno analizo zaviranja ter smernega nadzora.

Physics in brief

Fizika na kratko

The target is not to levitate a plane like a train. The conservative target is fractional lift on the landing gear, enough to reduce normal force and rolling losses while preserving contact, steering authority, suspension behavior, and safety margins.

Cilj ni, da letalo lebdi kot vlak. Konservativen cilj je delni dvig podvozja, dovolj za zmanjšanje normalne sile in kotalnih izgub, ob ohranitvi stika, krmiljenja, delovanja vzmetenja in varnostnih rezerv.

N₀ ≈ mg − Lₐ; λ = Fmag/N₀
P_roll,OFF ≈ f_r · N₀ · v
ΔP_roll ≈ λ · f_r · N₀ · v
Net benefit = rolling-loss reduction − added magnetic drag and system input
Decision test: On the same loaded wheel truck, compare MAT OFF and ON at matched speed and gap. Measure tire normal force, longitudinal drag, electrical input and heat. If the upper uncertainty bound of measured load relief stays below 5% across the intended range, that tested geometry misses the lower target. Positive lift alone does not establish net savings. Compare an optimized conventional tire and gear setup under the same load and grip requirements.
Odločilni preizkus: Na istem obremenjenem kolesnem vozičku primerjaj MAT izklopljen in vklopljen pri enaki hitrosti in reži. Izmeri normalno silo na pnevmatiki, upor v smeri vožnje, električni vhod in toploto. Če zgornja meja merilne negotovosti pri razbremenitvi ostane pod 5 % v predvidenem območju, preizkušena geometrija ne doseže spodnjega cilja. Sila navzgor sama še ne dokazuje neto prihranka. Pri enaki obremenitvi in zahtevah za oprijem primerjaj optimizirane običajne pnevmatike in podvozje.
  • Benefits scale with lift fraction λ and speed v during the assisted ground run.
  • A modest λ = 0.10 cuts rolling losses by about 10% in the assisted segment.
  • Fuel, distance, tire-wear and hot/high benefits are hypotheses requiring matched measurements. A brake-life benefit needs a separate mechanism and test.
  • Koristi rastejo z deležem dviga λ in hitrostjo v med podprtim delom pospeševanja.
  • Zmeren λ = 0.10 zmanjša kotalne izgube za približno 10% v podprtem segmentu.
  • Manjša poraba goriva, krajša razdalja, manjša obraba gum in večja rezerva v vročih/visokih pogojih so hipoteze za primerjalne meritve. Korist za zavore potrebuje ločen mehanizem in preizkus.

Dynamic clearance, safety and EMI

Dinamična razdalja, varnost in EMI

Clearance target

The proposed 1–3 cm dimension needs defined reference surfaces. Tire contact and per-wheel loads must be measured; software limits alone cannot establish a safe gap.

Smooth transitions

Overlapping zones and S-curve ramps are proposed to limit load steps; transition forces still need measurement.

Fault behavior

The design goal is a verified safe load transition for specified faults, including asymmetric loss and stuck-on fields. Decay time, allowable transients and pilot actions remain unknown.

EMI hygiene

Shielding, spectral planning, keep-out bands around ILS/GBAS, and continuous monitoring are non-negotiable certification gates.

Ciljna razdalja

Za predlaganih 1–3 cm je treba določiti referenčni površini. Izmeriti je treba stik pnevmatik in obremenitev vsakega kolesa; programske omejitve same ne dokazujejo varne reže.

Mehki prehodi

Prekrivajoče se cone in S-krivulje naj bi omejile skoke obremenitve; prehodne sile je treba izmeriti.

Obnašanje ob napaki

Cilj zasnove je dokazano varen prehod ob določenih napakah, tudi pri asimetrični izgubi in zataknjenem vklopu polja. Čas upada, dopustni sunki in potrebna dejanja pilota še niso znani.

EMI disciplina

Oklop, spektralno načrtovanje, varnostni pasovi okoli ILS/GBAS in stalni nadzor so nujni certifikacijski pogoji.

Safety gate: The plan targets confirmed OFF before V1, but rejected takeoffs can occur while assistance is active. Braking, steering and load transitions must be validated for both cases.
Varnostna vrata: Načrt predvideva potrjen izklop pred V1, vendar je zavrnjen vzlet mogoč tudi med aktivno pomočjo. Zaviranje, smerni nadzor in prehode obremenitev je treba preveriti v obeh primerih.

Phased adoption plan

Fazni načrt uvedbe

Phase A · Bench & track rigs

5–10 m lane; validate lift stability, thermal behavior, EMI, and loaded wheel-truck dynamics.

Phase B · Low-speed airfield pilot

300–500 m taxiway segment; one narrow-body main gear; prove ride quality and power-per-lift curves below 50 kt.

Phase C · High-speed ground runs

800–1000 m secondary strip; no-rotation runs; demonstrate fault-to-safe behavior and yaw/steer margins.

Phase D · Limited operations

One partner hub runway; a small number of scheduled departures per day; compare fuel, maintenance, safety, and reliability.

Faza A · Klopni in tirni testi

5–10 m pas; preverjanje stabilnosti dviga, toplote, EMI in dinamike obremenjenega kolesnega vozička.

Faza B · Nizkohitrostni pilot

300–500 m segment vozne steze; eno glavno podvozje ozkotrupnega letala; dokaz vožnje in krivulj moč/dvig pod 50 kt.

Faza C · Hitri zemeljski teki

800–1000 m sekundarne steze; brez rotacije; dokaz varnega izklopa in yaw/steer rezerv.

Faza D · Omejen operativni poskus

Ena partnerska steza; malo rednih odhodov na dan; primerjava goriva, vzdrževanja, varnosti in zanesljivosti.

What to measure

Kaj meriti

Fuel & thrust

Fuel burn, thrust settings, ambient conditions, accelerate-go and accelerate-stop distances against matched controls.

Maintenance

Tire and brake wear, rubber shed, FOD incidents, hub temperatures, and module service intervals.

Dynamics

Lateral deviation, tiller/rudder inputs, vibration spectra, ride transitions, and fault-to-safe behavior.

Reliability

Zone uptime, fault rate, time-to-safe-state, module MTTR, and degradation under weather cycles.

EMI

Avionics logs, radio link integrity, ILS/GBAS behavior, radar altimeter health, and airport-system interference.

Noise

Ground-run noise profile under assisted versus control departures.

Gorivo in potisk

Poraba goriva, nastavitve potiska, vreme, accelerate-go in accelerate-stop razdalje proti kontrolnim letom.

Vzdrževanje

Obraba pnevmatik in zavor, odpadanje gume, FOD dogodki, temperature pest in servisni intervali modulov.

Dinamika

Stransko odstopanje, vhodi tiller/rudder, vibracije, prehodi pri vožnji in obnašanje fault-to-safe.

Zanesljivost

Uptime con, stopnja napak, čas do varnega stanja, MTTR modulov in degradacija v vremenskih ciklih.

EMI

Logi avionike, integriteta radijskih povezav, obnašanje ILS/GBAS, radar altimeter in motnje letaliških sistemov.

Hrup

Profil hrupa pri pospeševanju po tleh za podprte in kontrolne odhode.

Open questions for peer review

Odprta vprašanja za strokovni pregled

  • Optimal lift fraction profiles by aircraft class.
  • Best hub-ring materials and geometry for brake-heat resilience.
  • Zone lengths versus EMI guard bands around site-specific navaids.
  • Net grid impact and local storage sizing for peak shaving.
  • Winterization: grooving, anti-ice chemistry, and friction retention on module covers.
  • Optimalni profili deleža dviga po razredu letala.
  • Najboljši materiali in geometrija obročev za odpornost ob toploti zavor.
  • Dolžine con v primerjavi z EMI varnostnimi pasovi okoli navigacijskih sistemov.
  • Vpliv na omrežje in velikost lokalnega hranilnika energije.
  • Zimska uporaba: grooving, kemija proti ledu in ohranitev trenja na pokrovih modulov.

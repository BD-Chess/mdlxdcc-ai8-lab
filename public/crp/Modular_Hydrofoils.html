The Modular Hydrofoil Revolution

Modularna hydrofoil revolucija

BD proposes two modular hydrofoil retrofits for selected 6–9 passenger boats. Comfort, efficiency and a safe return to hull mode are targets for prototype testing.

BD predlaga dva modularna kompleta hidrokril za izbrane čolne s 6–9 potniki. Udobje, učinkovitost in varen povratek v plovbo na trupu so cilji, ki jih mora preveriti prototip.

Short version

Kratek povzetek

Purpose-built hydrofoils proved that “flying above water” can be quiet, efficient, and comfortable. The limitation is cost and specialization. This concept proposes bolt-on, serviceable hydrofoil modules for existing small boats: a simpler Hybrid Front-Lift Kit and a more ambitious Full Hydrofoil Kit.

Namensko grajeni hydrofoil čolni so pokazali, da je “letenje nad vodo” lahko tiho, učinkovito in udobno. Omejitev sta cena in specializiranost. Ta koncept predlaga privijačene, servisabilne hydrofoil module za obstoječe manjše čolne: enostavnejši Hybrid Front-Lift Kit in ambicioznejši Full Hydrofoil Kit.

BD original framing: “Two retrofit kits that turn ordinary 6–9 passenger boats into smoother, more efficient, wave-skimming machines while preserving a safe fallback to classic hull mode.” This remains an untested target.

Izvirna BD-jeva formulacija: “Two retrofit kits that turn ordinary 6–9 passenger boats into smoother, more efficient, wave-skimming machines while preserving a safe fallback to classic hull mode.” To je še nepreverjen cilj.

BD's proposed contribution

Make hydrofoil comfort accessible through a serviceable retrofit on suitable hulls. Prior-art review of hydrofoil-assisted hulls is pending.

First test

Measure foil-and-strut lift and drag on an RC rig, then test a structurally surveyed 6–7 m hull with load cells and IMU logging. The RC rig screens designs; it cannot prove full-scale performance.

Failure mode

If mounts overload, the bow porpoises, debris strikes are too severe, or fuel/comfort gains are weak, redesign or stop.

BD-jev predlagani prispevek

Udobje hidrokril omogočiti s servisabilno predelavo primernih trupov. Pregled obstoječih čolnov s podporo hidrokril še čaka.

Prvi test

Na RC napravi izmeriti dvig in upor kril z nosilci, nato preizkusiti strukturno pregledan 6–7 m trup z merilniki obremenitve in IMU. RC naprava preseje zasnove; ne dokazuje zmogljivosti v polni velikosti.

Način padca

Če so nosilci preobremenjeni, bow porpoising premočan, udarci smeti prehudi ali prihranki/udobje šibki, se koncept spremeni ali ustavi.

Two modes, one retrofit philosophy

Dva načina, ena retrofit filozofija

Čoln + zložljivi foil moduli — poenostavljena skica

Concept sketch: two foil modules support a conventional boat hull. Structural load paths, foil depth and the return to hull mode must be tested; the sketch is not to scale.

Konceptna skica: dva modula hidrokril podpirata običajen trup. Preizkusiti je treba pot obremenitev, globino kril in povratek v plovbo na trupu; skica ni v merilu.

Kit A · Hybrid Front-Lift

Two forward-angled bow-mounted struts or inverted-V side modules aim to raise the bow 15–30 cm. The rear hull and motor stay in the water. This clearance is smaller than the provisional 1 m chop target; any impact reduction must be measured.

Kit B · Full Hydrofoil

Four adjustable poles lift the entire hull. Rear foils can integrate electric pod motors for thrust and control.

Kit B · Full Hydrofoil

Štirje nastavljivi nosilci dvignejo cel trup. Zadnji foili lahko vključijo električne pod motorje za potisk in nadzor.

Kit A — Hybrid Front-Lift

Kit A — Hybrid Front-Lift

The Hybrid kit targets the highest-pain part of small-boat cruising: bow slamming, spray, and uncomfortable impacts in chop. It raises only the bow while keeping propulsion conventional.

Hybrid kit cilja najbolj boleč del plovbe z manjšim čolnom: udarjanje premca, pršenje in neprijetne udarce v razburkani vodi. Dvigne samo premec, pogon pa ostane klasičen.

Mounts

Reinforced collars near the bow, tied into the anchor locker, hawse area, and transverse frames. Quick-release side brackets support serviceability.

Controls

Manual or electro-hydraulic control of foil depth, V-angle, attack angle, and optional rear trim plates to prevent porpoising.

Use case

Candidate uses include lakes, coastal hops and small working boats. Roughly 1 m chop is BD's provisional test target, not a validated operating limit; wave height definition, period, heading, load and speed still need limits.

Indicative targets

BD targets 10–20% less fuel per nautical mile at equal speed and load, 2–4 kn higher comfortable cruise in chop, and an installed kit around $25k–$60k. None is yet measured; include foil-and-strut drag and mounting cost.

Nosilci

Ojačani obroči pri premcu, povezani z anchor lockerjem, hawse območjem in prečnimi rebri. Hitro snemljivi stranski nosilci olajšajo servis.

Nadzor

Ročno ali elektro-hidravlično nastavljanje globine foila, V-kota, kota napada in opcijskih zadnjih trim plošč za preprečitev porpoisinga.

Uporaba

Možne uporabe so jezera, obalni prevozi in manjši delovni čolni. Približno 1 m valov je BD-jev začasni cilj preizkusa, ne potrjena meja uporabe; določiti je treba višino in periodo vala, smer, obremenitev ter hitrost.

Okvirni cilji

BD cilja na 10–20 % manj goriva na navtično miljo pri enaki hitrosti in obremenitvi, 2–4 kn višjo udobno hitrost v valovih ter nameščen komplet okoli $25k–$60k. Nič od tega še ni izmerjeno; vključiti je treba upor kril z nosilci in strošek vpetja.

First falsifier: Measure the effective lift-to-drag ratio of foils plus struts and the baseline hull's resistance at cruise. A foil that adds more drag than the hull load it removes cannot meet the fuel target. The final test alternates matched A/B runs and reports fuel per nautical mile, comfort acceleration and mounting loads with uncertainty.
Prva ovrgljiva številka: Izmeri dejansko razmerje dvig/upor kril z nosilci in upor osnovnega trupa pri potovalni hitrosti. Če krilo doda več upora, kot ga odvzeta obremenitev trupa prihrani, cilj porabe ni dosežen. Končni preizkus naj izmenjuje primerljive vožnje A/B ter z negotovostjo poroča gorivo na navtično miljo, pospeške in obremenitve vpetij.
Simple clever variant: a slightly forward-tilted deck at rest can become level when the bow rises under foil lift, reducing the need for active interior trim systems.
Preprosta pametna varianta: rahlo naprej nagnjen pod pri mirovanju lahko postane raven, ko se premec dvigne na foilih, brez aktivnih sistemov notranjega trima.

Kit B — Full Hydrofoil Kit

Kit B — Full Hydrofoil Kit

The Full kit is the ambitious version: the whole hull clears the water once take-off speed is reached. This is closer to the Candela/Navier lineage, but framed as a modular retrofit path.

Full kit je ambicioznejša verzija: cel trup se dvigne iz vode, ko čoln doseže hitrost vzleta. To je bližje smeri Candela/Navier, vendar kot modularna retrofit pot.

Control loop

IMU, GPS, pressure sensors, optional sonar/LiDAR wave prediction, and PID/LQR control for flap deflection and foil depth.

Propulsion

60–180 kW electric across one or two rear foil pods, with battery pack inboard and steer-by-wire yaw.

Use case

Candidate uses include low-wake leisure travel and sensitive waterways in calm to moderate seas. Controlled return to hull mode in larger seas is a safety requirement to demonstrate, including power loss and asymmetric deployment.

Indicative targets

BD targets 50–70% less energy per nautical mile at equal speed and load than the same hull in planing mode, with a proposed kit price around $90k–$180k including e-drive and battery. Range, battery mass, installed cost and savings are unverified.

Nadzorna zanka

IMU, GPS, tlačni senzorji, opcijski sonar/LiDAR za napoved vala ter PID/LQR nadzor krilc in globine foila.

Pogon

60–180 kW elektrike prek enega ali dveh zadnjih pod motorjev, z baterijo v trupu in steer-by-wire yaw nadzorom.

Uporaba

Možne uporabe so rekreacijska plovba z nizkim lastnim valom in občutljiva vodna območja v mirnem do zmernem morju. Nadzorovan povratek v plovbo na trupu v večjih valovih je varnostna zahteva za dokaz, tudi ob izgubi napajanja in nesimetričnem umiku.

Okvirni cilji

BD cilja na 50–70 % manj energije na navtično miljo pri enaki hitrosti in obremenitvi kot isti trup med glisiranjem, s predlagano ceno kompleta okoli $90k–$180k z električnim pogonom in baterijo. Doseg, masa baterije, vgrajeni strošek in prihranek še niso preverjeni.

Engineering notes

Inženirske opombe

Lift per foil: L = 1/2 · ρ · V² · S · C_L
Hybrid target: foils support ~20–30% of vessel weight; trim and moments must balance
Full target: total foil lift balances vessel weight, with a maneuver margin

Strut geometry

BD proposes forward-raked struts to seek lower drag and bending moments; neither follows from rake alone and both need geometry-specific load tests. V-angle could adjust depth, subject to ventilation and structural limits.

Materials

Carbon composite struts and foils, 316L or duplex stainless fasteners, sacrificial anodes, and isolation bushings for corrosion control.

Loads & safety

Design for slam, debris strikes, shock-mount pivots, overload shear pins, and auto-retract logic on impact spikes.

Geometrija nosilcev

BD predlaga naprej nagnjene nosilce za manjši upor in upogibni moment; nagib sam tega ne zagotovi, zato je potreben preizkus konkretne geometrije. V-kot bi lahko prilagajal globino ob upoštevanju ventilacije in strukturnih omejitev.

Materiali

Karbonski kompozitni nosilci in foili, 316L ali duplex nerjavni pritrdilni elementi, žrtvene anode in izolacijske puše proti koroziji.

Obremenitve in varnost

Zasnova mora pokriti slamming, udarce smeti, shock-mount pivote, varovalne shear-pine in samodejen umik ob sunkih.

Development plan

Razvojni načrt

1 · Simulate and size

CFD plus empirical lift curves for 6–9 passenger boats, including displacement, beam, speed, foil area, and mount loads.

2 · Scale demonstrator

RC catamaran to validate control laws, V-geometry, retraction, and rough-water response.

3 · Hybrid prototype

6–7 m center console with IMU, load cells, strain gauges, fuel logging, slamming acceleration, and comfort index.

4 · Full e-pod prototype

Rear pod propulsion, flight-control refinement, fault handling, sea trials, and pilot customers.

1 · Simulacija in dimenzioniranje

CFD plus empirične krivulje dviga za 6–9 potniške čolne, z izpodrivom, širino, hitrostjo, površino foilov in obremenitvami nosilcev.

2 · Merilni demonstrator

RC katamaran za validacijo nadzornih zakonov, V-geometrije, umika in odziva v valovih.

3 · Hybrid prototip

6–7 m center console z IMU, merilniki obremenitve, strain gauges, logom goriva, slamming pospeškom in indeksom udobja.

4 · Full e-pod prototip

Zadnji pod pogon, izboljšanje flight-control sistema, obravnava napak, morski testi in pilotni kupci.

Markets and collaboration

Trgi in sodelovanje

Retrofit comfort

Family and fishing boats get less slamming and more comfortable cruising without replacing the vessel.

Rescue and patrol

Faster response in chop, retractable fallback in surf, and modular serviceability.

Eco-tourism

Quiet, lower-wake operation near sensitive shorelines.

Superyacht tenders

Luxury ride upgrade without replacing the tender fleet.
